# Kuwait SEO and Local-Search Launch Plan

This checklist covers work that cannot be completed through website code alone. It does not promise rankings, enquiries or inclusion in AI-generated search results.

## Before launch

1. Create or claim Zenith Line Co.'s Google Business Profile and complete verification through the method Google offers.
2. Confirm that the public business name, address, primary phone, website and customer-facing location status exactly match real-world records and permanent signage.
3. Select the most accurate primary category and only relevant secondary categories. Do not add categories merely to target keywords.
4. Add factual service descriptions and genuine photographs of the premises, team, equipment and approved work. Record ownership and permission for every image.
5. Confirm whether the Mirqab address receives customers during stated business hours or should be configured as a service-area business.
6. Confirm real operating hours, service areas and any temporary closures before publishing them.

## Reputation and local authority

7. Ask genuine clients for honest reviews without incentives, suggested ratings or fabricated wording.
8. Respond professionally to positive and negative reviews, referring to the specific feedback without exposing confidential project information.
9. Build consistent citations in legitimate Kuwait business directories and relevant industry sources. Keep the company name, address, phone and website identical.
10. Pursue editorially legitimate links from suppliers, project partners, professional associations and relevant Kuwait directories. Never purchase spam backlinks or create fake reviews.

## Indexing and measurement

11. Verify the production domain in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools, then submit `https://zenithlinekw.com/sitemap-index.xml`.
12. Inspect and request indexing for `/services/telecom-it/` and its ten service pages after the production release is live and crawlable.
13. Record a pre-launch baseline for indexed pages, impressions, queries, average positions, organic clicks, engaged visits and WhatsApp conversions.
14. Track service-level search queries and conversions with Search Console and analytics. Separate branded queries from service-intent queries where practical.
15. Review results after 30, 60 and 90 days. Improve pages from real query, conversion and enquiry-quality evidence rather than adding speculative keywords or location pages.

## Information required from Zenith Line

- Confirm the exact Google Business Profile name and whether the Mirqab office is customer-facing with permanent signage.
- Confirm business hours and genuine Kuwait service coverage.
- Confirm the preferred primary and secondary Google Business Profile categories.
- Supply approved project photographs, captions, locations, dates, sectors and usage permissions.
- Supply evidence for any registration, certification, manufacturer relationship, maintenance agreement, response-time or SLA claim before publication.
- Confirm a responsible owner for Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools, analytics, Business Profile access and monthly reporting.

Google's current guidance should be checked again when the profile is configured, because platform policies and available categories can change.
